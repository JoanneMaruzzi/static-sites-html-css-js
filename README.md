# static-sites-html-css-js
A practical guide to assessing and improving a rural business’s online presence. Learn to audit visibility, build trust, measure enquiries and sales, and create affordable static websites on CDN hosting. Includes a 90-day action plan, visual scorecards, and ideas for publishing a business news site.

1. **A structured assessment method.** The article explains how to evaluate a rural business’s online presence across six areas: discoverability, information accuracy, trust, mobile usability, customer conversion, and maintenance readiness.

2. **An understanding of rural customers.** It shows how customer interviews and practical journey tests can reveal problems with directions, service coverage, opening hours, connectivity, and contact options.

3. **Measurable business outcomes.** The methodology connects website activity to enquiries, qualified leads, completed sales, and contribution. Illustrative calculations explain how to assess improvement without confusing traffic with commercial success.

4. **Affordable static website options.** The article describes how HTML, CSS, and optional JavaScript can support a useful business website on CDN hosting. It discusses Cloudflare Pages and Netlify, including free-plan limits and potential additional costs.

5. **A static news publishing model.** Using Market for Profits as a reference for the publishing concept, the article explains how individual story pages, category archives, search, RSS feeds, and reusable templates can form a static news site.

6. **Supporting visual examples.** Two photographs illustrate business information management and product photography. Two diagrams explain the assessment scorecard and the customer enquiry-to-sale funnel, with hypothetical figures clearly identified.

7. **A practical ninety-day action plan.** The article organizes improvements into manageable stages, from correcting information and repairing contact channels to testing changes, reviewing results, and planning ongoing maintenance.
